# Architecture Decision Records (ADRs)

This directory contains Architecture Decision Records documenting significant architectural decisions made during the development of SmartHealth.

## ADR Index

| ADR | Title | Status | Date |
|-----|-------|--------|------|
| 001 | Layered Architecture with FastAPI | Proposed | 2026 |

## Template

New ADRs should follow the structure in `001-architecture-decision-record.md` and include:

- **Title** — Short descriptive title
- **Status** — Proposed, Accepted, Deprecated, Superseded
- **Context** — The problem or situation motivating the decision
- **Decision** — The chosen approach
- **Consequences** — Positive and negative outcomes
- **Alternatives Considered** — Other options evaluated and why they were rejected
